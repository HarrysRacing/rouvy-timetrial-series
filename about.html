About Harry's Race Series

Harry's Race Series is a community-driven ROUVY time trial series that combines individual race events into a season-long standings competition.

Riders compete across a series of stages, earning points based on their performance. General Classification standings are updated after each stage, allowing participants to track their progress throughout the season.

With Harry's Race Series the focus is on fair competition, transparent results, and an enjoyable racing experience for riders of all abilities.
