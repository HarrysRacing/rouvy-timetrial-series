Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this community race series site collects, uses, and stores data obtained through the ROUVY API and related services.

Overview

This site is an independent community project designed to aggregate race results and publish standings for indoor cycling events.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by ROUVY.

Data Collected

Depending on the API endpoints used, the site may collect and process:

  • Race event identifiers and metadata
  • Participant display names
  • Event rankings and finish times
  • Activity summary metrics
  • Performance aggregates such as power, cadence, heart rate, speed, or elevation statistics

The site does not collect or store rider passwords or ROUVY login credentials.

Source of Data

Data displayed on this site is retrieved through authorised access to the ROUVY API using OAuth2 authentication.

How Data Is Used

Data is used solely for purposes related to operation of the race series, including:

  • Computing standings and rankings
  • Displaying event results
  • Generating statistics and summaries
  • Maintaining historical race records

Data Retention

Race result and standings data may be retained for historical and statistical purposes related to the operation of the race series.

Raw API responses may also be temporarily stored for auditing, recalculation, troubleshooting, or validation purposes.

Data Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Public standings and event results may be displayed openly on the site as part of normal race series operation.

Cookies and Analytics

This site may use basic web hosting logs or analytics tools to understand site usage and improve reliability. No advertising trackers are intentionally used.

Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect API credentials and stored data from unauthorised access.

However, no internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

External Links

This site may contain links to third-party websites or services. The operator is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically as the project evolves. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the current version.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or privacy concerns may be directed to the project maintainer via the published project contact information.
