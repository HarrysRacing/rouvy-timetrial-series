Rider Registration


Registration simply connects your ROUVY account to Harrys Racing Series so that your rides can be included in race standings.

  • To register for Harrys Racing Series you must be an active subscriber to the ROUVY indoor cycling platform.
  • Registration is completed securely through ROUVY.
  • By selecting the registration link below, you will be redirected to ROUVY to authenticate your account and authorize Harry's Racing Series access.
  • Harrys Racing Series does not receive or store your ROUVY login credentials, including your password. Authentication is performed entirely by ROUVY.
  • Following successful authorization, your ROUVY profile and relevant activity data may be accessed through the ROUVY API solely for the purpose of managing race participation, calculating results, and displaying standings.
  • Further details regarding the Harrys Racing Series Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy are available via the links on the Home page.
