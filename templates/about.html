About

Harrys Racing Series website is an independent community resource that schedules races on the ROUVY indoor virtual cycling platform and uses data from this platform to aggregate event results and compute standings to enable registered riders to participate in a a race series.

This site publishes race event details, schedules, participants, and results for a structured indoor cycling series.

Harrys Racing Series is not affiliated with ROUVY and operates as a community-driven initiative.

What this site does

  • Displays race event details, schedules, and participants
  • Displays race event results and standings
  • Aggregates results across a race series
  • Provides transparent ranking updates
