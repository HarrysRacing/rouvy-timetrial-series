About

Harrys Racing Series is a community-based competition and results website for events conducted on the ROUVY indoor cycling platform. Races are scheduled and run on the ROUVY platform and event results data is used to compute both Stage and General Classification standings, which are then published on this website.

Harrys Racing Series is not affiliated with ROUVY and operates as a community-driven initiative.

What this site does

  • Displays race event details, schedules, and participants
  • Displays race event results and standings
  • Aggregates results across a race series
  • Provides transparent ranking updates
