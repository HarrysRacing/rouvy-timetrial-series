About


Harrys Racing website was created by me, Old-Harry, as a place to record race times, publish race results and calculate a General Classification ranking for the Harrys Racing time trial events which I organise on ROUVY.

I started running time trial events on ROUVY for fun, just over a year ago (in mid-2025) to see what, if any, interest it would generate. And, over the course of three time trial series', close to three hundred ROUVY riders took part in the Harrys TT events.

Back then, another website tracked race results and overall standings. That other site shutdown in March 2026, and since then I have spent a large part of my spare time developing this new site as a dedicated home just for Harrys Racing Series.

My aim in creating this website is to add some value to the TT races that I run on ROUVY, and to foster some friendly competition amongst those of us who enjoy testing ourselves against others, as well as ourselves, in the race of truth.

Hopefully you all get some fun in seeing where you stand against others over a series of races, as well as how you improve your own race efforts for yourself.

I look forward to seeing you on the ROUVY road and, if you haven't already, please do "friend" me and as well, join the Harrys Racing Community on ROUVY.

Old-Harry
